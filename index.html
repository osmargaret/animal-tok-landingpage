Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026  |  Effective Date: November 16, 2026  |  Open dedicated page ↗

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, username, and password (stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing). We never store plaintext passwords.

Profile & Media: Profile photos, cover banners, pet details, videos, images, comments, likes, and bookmarks you create on Animandler.

Device & Telemetry: Device type, OS version, and app interaction data to optimize video streaming performance. We do not track your continuous background GPS location.

Payments: Marketplace transactions and paid event bookings are processed securely by Paystack. Animandler never stores your card credentials.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

We use your data solely to deliver the Animandler experience: powering your personalized video feed, enabling social interactions, managing marketplace listings, and sending authentication OTP emails via Resend.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS, and all database records are secured via Neon PostgreSQL SSL.

3. Data Retention & User Rights (Account Deletion)

You retain full control over your personal information. You have the right to access, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated posts, media, comments, and profile data at any time.

To delete your account, use the in-app profile settings or submit a request directly on our Account Deletion Request Page. Requests are processed within 30 days.

For questions about our privacy practices, contact our team at support@animandler.com.

1. Acceptance & Eligibility

By creating an account or using Animandler, you agree to these Terms. You must be at least 13 years old to use the platform. Each individual may maintain only one personal account.

Animandler strictly prohibits any content depicting harm, neglect, abuse, exploitation, fighting, or cruelty toward animals. Any accounts posting or promoting such material will be permanently banned immediately and reported to law enforcement authorities.

Content on Animandler is for educational and entertainment purposes and does not replace in-person veterinary medical examination. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for urgent medical concerns.
