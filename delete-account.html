Account & Data Deletion

Submit a request to permanently delete your Animandler account and all associated data.

⚠️

This action is permanent and irreversible

Once your deletion request is processed, your account and all data cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

When we process your deletion request, we will permanently remove:

  • ✓ Your profile (name, username, email, bio, avatar, cover photo)
  • ✓ All your posts (videos, images, captions)
  • ✓ All your comments and replies
  • ✓ All your likes and bookmarks
  • ✓ Your followers and following lists
  • ✓ Your messages and conversations
  • ✓ Your marketplace listings and purchase history
  • ✓ All device tokens and session data

Note: Basic transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial regulations. These records will not be linked to your identity in any publicly accessible way.

How Long It Takes

Your deletion request will be processed within 30 days of submission. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete. During this period, your account will be deactivated and invisible to other users.

If you log back in during the 30-day window, your deletion request will be cancelled and your account will be restored.

Alternative: Delete From the App

You can also delete your account directly from within the Animandler app:

  1. Go to your Profile tab
  2. Tap the Settings (⚙️) icon
  3. Scroll to Account section
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Follow the confirmation steps

Submit a Deletion Request

Fill out the form below and we will process your request within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email.

Alternatively, email us directly at support@animandler.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Questions?

If you have any concerns about your data or the deletion process, contact our support team:

  • Email: support@animandler.app
  • Response time: Within 5 business days

For more information about how we handle your data, please read our Privacy Policy.
