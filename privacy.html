Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026  |  Effective Date: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Animandler ("we," "our," or "us"). Animandler is a short-video and community platform built for pet lovers, animal handlers, and veterinary professionals. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"), how we use it, with whom we share it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the terms of this policy.

If you have questions, contact us at support@animandler.app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

A. Account Information

When you register, we collect your full name, email address, username, and password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we never store plain-text passwords). You may also optionally provide a bio, location, and professional title.

B. Profile Content

You may upload a profile photo (avatar) and cover image. These are stored securely via Cloudinary (our media CDN).

C. User-Generated Content

Any videos, images, captions, comments, likes, bookmarks, and marketplace listings you create or interact with are stored and associated with your account.

D. Usage and Device Data

We automatically collect limited technical information including device type, operating system version, app version, and in-app interactions (e.g., screens viewed, features used). We do not collect precise GPS location.

E. Communication Data

When you request a One-Time Password (OTP) for account verification or login, we send a transactional email via Resend. We retain a log of sent emails for security and troubleshooting purposes.

F. Payment Data

If you use our Marketplace or service-booking features, payment processing is handled entirely by Paystack. Animandler does not store your card numbers or sensitive payment credentials. We only receive confirmation of successful transactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account — authentication, profile display, and security.
  • Deliver and personalize the feed — showing you content from creators and pets you follow.
  • Enable community features — likes, comments, real-time chat, and notifications.
  • Power the Marketplace — listing pet products and services, processing orders via Paystack.
  • Send essential communications — OTP verification emails (no marketing emails without consent).
  • Improve the Service — analyzing usage patterns to fix bugs and develop new features.
  • Enforce our policies — detecting and preventing fraud, spam, and harmful content.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

4. Information We Share

We share your data only with the following trusted service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Cloudinary — Media storage and delivery (CDN) for all uploaded photos and videos.
  • Neon PostgreSQL — Managed cloud database (hosted in US-East region) where your account data, posts, and interactions are stored.
  • Render — Cloud hosting provider for our backend API servers.
  • Resend — Transactional email provider for OTP and account notification emails.
  • Paystack — Payment processor for marketplace and service-booking transactions. Subject to Paystack's own privacy policy.
  • Socket.io — Real-time messaging infrastructure embedded in our backend.

All providers are contractually obligated to use your data only to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: We retain your data for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Upon a confirmed deletion request, we permanently delete your profile, posts, comments, media, and personal identifiers within 30 days.
  • Payment records: Basic transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by financial and tax regulations.
  • Security logs: Limited logs (login attempts, OTP events) may be retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information via your profile settings in the app.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data via our Account Deletion page or by emailing support@animandler.app.
  • Data Portability: Request a machine-readable export of your data.
  • Withdraw Consent: You may delete your account at any time to withdraw your consent to data processing.
  • Opt-Out of Communications: Since we only send transactional emails (OTP), there are no marketing emails to unsubscribe from at this time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@animandler.app. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Animandler is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, please contact us immediately at support@animandler.app and we will promptly delete the account and all associated data.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — they are never stored in plain text.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Database connections use Neon SSL encryption.
  • Media files are stored on Cloudinary with access control policies.

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, please change your password immediately and contact us.

9. International Data Transfers

Animandler is operated from the United States. Our database is hosted in the US-East region. If you are accessing the Service from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify active users through an in-app notification. Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out to us:

  • Email: support@animandler.app
  • Platform: Animandler Mobile App (Android)

We aim to respond to all privacy inquiries within 30 business days.
